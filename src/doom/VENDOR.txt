Vendored from https://github.com/ozkl/doomgeneric (Chocolate Doom / Linux Doom).

Local changes for ESP32 VGA32:
- doomfeatures.h: FEATURE_SOUND disabled
- config.h: FILES_DIR=/sd, unistd/stat available
- i_system.c: zone malloc from PSRAM, I_Error does not call exit/zenity
- doomgeneric.c: screen buffer from PSRAM
- m_config.c: default config dir /sd
- Large renderer/info/ticcmd tables allocated from PSRAM (Arduino
  linker cannot place .ext_ram.bss in PSRAM)
- Video backend is FabGL VGA16Controller (16-color packed framebuffer)

Platform backends (SDL, X11, Win32, …) were not copied.
The FabGL backend lives in src/doomgeneric_fabgl.cpp.
